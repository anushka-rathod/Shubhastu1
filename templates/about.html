The Heritage of Shubhashtu

"Style That Speaks for You."

Founded with a passion for Indian art and contemporary design, Shubhashtu Fashion bridges royal heritage with modern everyday elegance.

Artisan Roots

Born from India's Rich Artistic Soil

India's artisanal heritage spans thousands of years — from the royal Kundan ateliers of Jaipur to the fine needlecraft of Lucknow and the temple stone carvings of Tamil Nadu.

Shubhashtu Fashion was founded on a simple yet profound conviction: that traditional craftsmanship should not remain confined to museums or bridal heirlooms, but should flourish in the modern wardrobe with grace, comfort, and uncompromising elegance.

Wearable Art

Individually Painted T-Shirts

In an era of mass-produced fast fashion, we chose slow, intentional creation. Each of our Hand-Painted T-Shirts is treated as an individual canvas.

Our artists spend hours hand-painting peacocks, sacred mandalas, and royal motifs using permanent, skin-friendly fabric acrylics on pure 220 GSM bio-washed organic cotton. No two pieces are ever completely identical — making yours truly one-of-a-kind.

Our Guiding Pillars

Purity of Material

Hypoallergenic brass, 24K micron plating, hand-cut Kundan stones, and 100% breathable organic cotton.

Fair Artisan Wages

Direct partnerships with master jewelry karigars and painters ensuring dignified wages and family livelihoods.

Delightful Experience

All-India fast courier dispatch, luxury satin packaging, and round-the-clock WhatsApp support.
