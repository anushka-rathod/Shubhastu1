How to Connect Your Razorpay Account

  1. Visit Razorpay Dashboard and sign in.
  2. In the left sidebar, click Settings → API Keys.
  3. Click Generate Key. You will receive a Key ID and Key Secret.
  4. Copy and paste them into the fields above, then click Save Payment Settings.
  5. Ensure your store domain is active or tested. Real payments will now be verified securely!
