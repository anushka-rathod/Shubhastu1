Shipping & Delivery Policy

• Serviceable Locations: We deliver to over 19,000+ PIN codes across India through premier courier partners including BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC, and India Post.

• Free Delivery: All prepaid and COD orders with a net cart value of ₹999 and above receive complimentary standard shipping.

• Standard Delivery Charge: A nominal delivery fee of ₹70 is applicable on orders below ₹999.

• Dispatch Timelines: In-stock jewelry and ready pieces are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours. Individually hand-painted t-shirts require 2 business days for meticulous drying and packaging.

• Estimated Delivery Duration:
  - Metro Cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata): 2 to 4 business days.
  - Tier-1 & State Capitals: 3 to 5 business days.
  - Rest of India: 4 to 6 business days.

• Tracking: Real-time AWB courier tracking links are provided via SMS, WhatsApp, and the Track Order portal.

Return & Refund Policy

• 7-Day Return & Exchange Window: You may initiate a return or size exchange request within 7 calendar days from the date of package delivery.

• Eligibility Criteria: Items must be in their original condition, unworn, unwashed, with all original tags attached, and in the protective Shubhashtu satin/velvet pouch.

• Return Pickup: We arrange reverse door-step pickup across all serviceable locations in India free of charge for size exchanges.

• Refund Processing: Once the returned parcel passes inspection at our studio:
  - Prepaid Orders: Refund is automatically credited back to your original payment source (UPI/Card/Bank) within 3-5 business days.
  - Cash on Delivery: Refund is transferred to your provided UPI ID or bank account via NEFT within 2 business days.

Privacy Policy

• Shubhashtu Fashion complies with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).
